The three ideas behind Img.Maze

Everything on the Landing, Water and Desert pages is one of these three ideas, repeated with more pictures. Open lab.css next to this page. Change a number, save, refresh, and watch what moves.

1. Stacking: who is in front

Hover a square. Landing page: every piece is placed with position:absolute and stacked with z-index.

One number scales everything. Landing page: the pile's font-size, with every piece sized in em.

2. Hover one thing, change another

Point at the blue square, then move away. Water page: the corrupted patch reveals the hidden creature.

3. Click one thing, change another (and start an animation)

Click the sun. Click it again. Desert page: the sun is a label for a hidden checkbox.
